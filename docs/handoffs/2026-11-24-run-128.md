## Finding and implementation

`CosmosDriverRideReservationStore.GetActiveForDriversAsync` previously called `DriverIdBatching.Chunk`, which eagerly enumerated and materialized all requested Driver IDs without observing the request cancellation token. A large or custom enumerable could continue after cancellation and delay query creation.

A cancellation-aware `DriverIdBatching.Chunk(IEnumerable<string>, CancellationToken)` overload now checks cancellation before each enumerated ID and after the final `MoveNext` reports end-of-input. The Cosmos store passes the request token into the helper. Cancellation during or at completion of enumeration throws before `Container.GetItemQueryIterator` is called. Ordinal de-duplication and 100-ID batch boundaries are unchanged.
